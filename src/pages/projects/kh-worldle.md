I built this daily game for the Kingdom Hearts community so that people had a GeoGuessr-ish game they could play daily to nerd out about the video game series.

The site is built using NextJS and uses screenshots provided by friends to supply the data set.

The game has been improved thanks to community feedback, and from what I hear, people are really enjoying it!
